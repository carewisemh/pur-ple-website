Who we are

✳

A community of purposeful people.

Purpose grows when we put it into practice.

Pur_Ple stands for Purposeful People. We bring people together around a simple belief: purpose grows when people are encouraged, supported and given opportunities to make a difference.

Founded by Pastor Tomi Usen in 2021, Pur_Ple brings care into practice through outreach, mentorship, giving and community service in Ile-Ife, Osun State, Nigeria.

We seek to respond to real community needs while encouraging people to recognise the potential within themselves and the communities around them.
